# How to Select the Right Water Pump

Selecting the right water pump is crucial for ensuring efficient operation and long service life. This comprehensive guide covers the 5 key parameters you need to consider when choosing a pump for your application.

## 1. Flow Rate (Q)

Flow rate is the volume of water the pump can move per unit of time, typically measured in cubic meters per hour (m³/h) or liters per minute (L/min).

**How to determine your required flow rate:**
- **Domestic water supply**: 1-3 m³/h per household
- **Garden irrigation**: 2-5 m³/h depending on area
- **Agricultural irrigation**: 5-50 m³/h depending on crop and area
- **Industrial processes**: Varies significantly by application

## 2. Head (H)

Head is the maximum height the pump can lift water, measured in meters. It includes:

- **Static head**: Vertical distance from water source to discharge point
- **Friction loss**: Pressure loss due to pipe friction (typically 10-20% of static head)
- **Pressure requirement**: Required pressure at the discharge point

**Rule of thumb**: Total head = Static head + Friction loss + Required pressure head

## 3. Pump Type Selection

Choose the right pump type based on your application:

| Application | Recommended Pump Type |
|------------|----------------------|
| Domestic pressure boosting | Peripheral Pump or VFD Pump |
| Clean water transfer | Centrifugal Pump |
| Deep well extraction | Deep Well Submersible Pump |
| Well water supply | Self-Priming Jet Pump |
| Wastewater/Drainage | Submersible Sewage Pump |
| High-rise water supply | Multi-Stage Pump Sets |
| Remote/Off-grid | Solar Pump System |

## 4. Material Selection

- **Cast Iron**: Economical, suitable for clean water
- **Stainless Steel (304/316)**: Corrosion-resistant, for aggressive water
- **Bronze/Brass**: Marine and saltwater applications
- **Plastic/Composite**: Light-duty, chemical transfer

## 5. Motor Power Calculation

Motor power depends on flow rate, head, and pump efficiency:

```
P (kW) = Q × H × ρ × g / (3.6 × 10⁶ × η)
```

Where:
- Q = Flow rate (m³/h)
- H = Head (m)
- ρ = Fluid density (1000 kg/m³ for water)
- η = Pump efficiency (typically 0.5-0.85)
