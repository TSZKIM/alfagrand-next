# ALFAGRAND at Water Expo Middle East 2026

ALFAGRAND successfully participated in the Water Expo Middle East 2026 held in Dubai, UAE, showcasing our comprehensive range of water pump solutions to the global market.

## Exhibition Highlights

Our booth attracted over 500 visitors from more than 30 countries, demonstrating the growing global interest in ALFAGRAND products. We showcased 30+ products across all our major categories:

- Peripheral and Centrifugal Pumps
- Deep Well Submersible Pumps
- Solar Pump Systems
- Variable Frequency Drive Pumps
- Multi-Stage Pump Sets

## New Partnerships

The exhibition was a tremendous success in terms of business development. We established new partnerships with distributors from 8 countries, including:

- United Arab Emirates
- Saudi Arabia
- Kuwait
- Qatar
- Oman
- Jordan
- Egypt
- Iraq

## Live Demonstrations

Our engineering team conducted live demonstrations of our solar pump systems and VFD pump controllers, giving visitors hands-on experience with our latest technology. The demonstrations highlighted the energy efficiency and smart control features of our new product lines.

## Looking Ahead

The success at Water Expo Middle East 2026 reinforces ALFAGRAND's commitment to the Middle East market. We look forward to serving our new partners and continuing to expand our presence in the region.
