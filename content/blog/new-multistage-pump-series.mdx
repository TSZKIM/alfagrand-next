# ALFAGRAND Launches New High-Efficiency Multistage Pump Series

ALFAGRAND is proud to announce the launch of our new high-efficiency multistage pump series, designed to meet the growing demand for energy-efficient water supply solutions in commercial and industrial applications.

## Industry-Leading Efficiency

Our latest multistage pump series achieves an industry-leading efficiency of 87%, representing a significant breakthrough in pump hydraulic design. Through advanced computational fluid dynamics (CFD) optimization, we've reduced energy consumption by up to 15% compared to previous generations.

## Product Range

The new series is available in 8 models with flow rates from 30 to 200 L/min, covering a wide range of applications:

- **Light Commercial**: 30-80 L/min models for small buildings and residential complexes
- **Mid-Range**: 80-150 L/min for medium-sized commercial buildings
- **Heavy-Duty**: 150-200 L/min for industrial and large-scale applications

## Key Features

- **Stainless Steel Construction**: All wetted parts in AISI 304 stainless steel for superior corrosion resistance
- **IE3 Premium Efficiency Motors**: Complying with international energy efficiency standards
- **Cartridge Mechanical Seal**: Easy maintenance and replacement
- **Smart Control Ready**: Compatible with VFD controllers for constant pressure systems

The new multistage pump series is now available for order worldwide. Contact our sales team for detailed specifications and pricing information.
