# 5 Essential Pump Maintenance Tips

Proper maintenance can extend your pump's service life by 5–10 years and prevent costly downtime. Here are five essential maintenance practices recommended by our engineering team.

## 1. Regular Vibration Monitoring

Unusual vibration is often the first sign of a developing problem. Establish a baseline vibration reading when the pump is new, and monitor periodically.

**What to check:**
- Bearing condition (worn bearings cause high-frequency vibration)
- Impeller balance (unbalanced impellers cause low-frequency vibration)
- Misalignment (shaft misalignment causes axial vibration)
- Cavitation (creates distinctive noise and vibration pattern)

**Recommended frequency**: Monthly for continuous-duty pumps, quarterly for intermittent use.

## 2. Mechanical Seal Inspection

The mechanical seal is one of the most critical components of any pump. Regular inspection prevents leaks and pump failure.

**Inspection checklist:**
- Check for visible leakage around the seal area
- Monitor seal flush flow and temperature
- Inspect seal faces for wear, scoring, or cracking
- Verify proper lubrication of the seal

**Warning signs**: Dripping water, staining around seal housing, unusual noise during operation.

## 3. Lubrication Best Practices

Proper lubrication reduces friction, prevents overheating, and extends bearing life.

**Guidelines:**
- Use manufacturer-recommended grease or oil
- Avoid over-greasing (causes overheating)
- Follow the recommended re-greasing schedule
- Keep grease fittings clean to prevent contamination
- Monitor bearing temperature (abnormal rise indicates lubrication issues)

## 4. Regular Performance Testing

Periodic performance testing helps identify efficiency loss before it becomes a major problem.

**Key measurements:**
- Flow rate (compare to pump curve)
- Discharge pressure
- Power consumption (increased consumption = efficiency loss)
- Suction pressure/vacuum

Document all readings and track trends over time. A gradual decrease in flow rate or increase in power consumption indicates wear.

## 5. Seasonal Preparation

For pumps that operate seasonally (irrigation, pool pumps), proper preparation for idle periods is essential.

**Before storage:**
- Drain all water to prevent freezing damage
- Clean internal passages to remove debris
- Apply rust inhibitor to exposed metal surfaces
- Cover or store in a dry location

**Before restart:**
- Rotate shaft manually to ensure free movement
- Check and refill lubrication
- Test run briefly without load
- Verify all connections and seals
