# ISO 9001:2015 Certification Renewed

ALFAGRAND is pleased to announce the successful renewal of our ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certification, following a rigorous annual audit conducted by an accredited third-party certification body.

## Zero Non-Conformities

This year's audit was particularly significant as we achieved **zero non-conformities** across all audited areas, demonstrating our unwavering commitment to quality excellence. The audit covered:

- Design and development processes
- Raw material procurement and inspection
- Manufacturing and assembly operations
- Testing and quality control procedures
- Customer service and after-sales support
- Continuous improvement initiatives

## What This Means for Our Customers

ISO 9001:2015 certification ensures that every ALFAGRAND pump is manufactured under strict quality control standards. Our customers can trust that:

- Every product undergoes 100% performance testing before shipment
- Raw materials are sourced from qualified, audited suppliers
- Manufacturing processes are standardized and documented
- Continuous improvement is embedded in our company culture

## Our Quality Commitment

Quality is not just a certification for ALFAGRAND — it is the foundation of everything we do. From design to delivery, we maintain rigorous standards to ensure every pump that leaves our factory meets the highest international quality requirements.

The renewal of our ISO 9001:2015 certification reinforces our position as a trusted water pump manufacturer serving customers in over 50 countries worldwide.
